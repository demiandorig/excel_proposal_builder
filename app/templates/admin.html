Disclaimers

Banner callouts added automatically to the Excel and PowerPoint exports when a plan mentions certain keywords or runs in a certain season. They sit below the totals and above the legal footer. Keywords match whole words or phrases (case-insensitive) against the client, geo, goal, targeting, products, and per-line notes of each option — so "live sports" matches "NBC Live Sports Stream", but "sport" won't match "sports".

Restrictions

Entravision's "Restricted Verticals by Platforms" Google Sheet is the source of truth for whether a client vertical (Political, Cannabis, Casino/Gambling…) is allowed on each product. The Roadblocks step reads it, computes a verdict per product, and the AI isn't allowed to contradict it. Sync it here whenever the sheet changes. You can also add your own categories and extra rules below: Step 07 offers them as choices and the AI reads them exactly like a sheet tab.
